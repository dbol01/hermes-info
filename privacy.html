Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 November 2026

Hermes is a personal automation assistant operated by a single individual (the domain owner) on their own home server, for use with their own Google account. This policy describes exactly how that Google user data is accessed, used and stored.

What data is accessed

With the account owner's OAuth consent, Hermes accesses the owner's own Google account data only: Gmail messages and labels; Calendar events; Drive/Sheets/Docs files; Tasks; and basic profile information needed to identify the account. No other user's data is ever requested.

How data is used

  • Summarising the owner's mail and calendar for the owner's own consumption.
  • Drafting and sending email on the owner's behalf, at the owner's direction.
  • Reading and maintaining household planning documents (Sheets/Docs) and reminders.

Where data is stored

All processed and stored data remains on the owner's self-hosted home server. Google user data is never sold, rented, shared with, or transferred to any third party, and is not used for advertising or for training machine-learning or AI models.

Retention and deletion

Email and calendar data are read transiently and are not permanently archived. OAuth credentials may be revoked at any time from Google Account → Security → Third-party access, which stops all access immediately; any locally cached credentials are deleted on revocation.

Contact

The owner of this site and app is the sole operator. Contact via the user-support email listed on the app's Google Cloud Console consent screen.
