Hermes — Personal Automation

Hermes is a single-user, personal automation assistant run by the owner of this domain on their own home server. It is not distributed, not sold, and not available to other users.

What it does

With the account owner's explicit OAuth authorization, Hermes reads and manages only the owner's own Google account data:

  • Gmail — triage of unread mail, drafting replies, labelling and archiving.
  • Google Calendar — surfacing upcoming events and creating reminders for the household.
  • Google Drive, Sheets and Docs — locating and maintaining family planning documents.
  • Google Tasks — keeping a personal task list in sync.

It never accesses data belonging to any other Google account, and no data is shared with third parties. All data stays on hardware owned and operated by the account owner.
